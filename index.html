Day trips from Madrid

Chosen: Segovia for a 9- and 12-year-old. The others are good if you change your mind.

Chosen

Segovia

~1 h drive

A 2,000-year-old Roman aqueduct you can walk right up to, and the Alcázar, the castle said to have inspired Disney's. Roast lamb for lunch.

GoogleApple

Toledo

~45 min drive

The closest and most dramatic: a whole medieval city on a hill, huge cathedral, sword and armour shops, marzipan. Steeper to walk.

GoogleApple

El Escorial

~50 min drive

A colossal royal monastery-palace in the cooler hills northwest of Madrid. Grand and shady on a scorching day. Least fun of the four for kids.

GoogleApple

Ávila

~1 h 15 drive

The most complete medieval walls in Europe: walk the whole way around on the ramparts. Great for kids who like to climb. A bit farther.

GoogleApple

Day trips from Barcelona

All easy with the car you keep the whole trip. Chosen: Montserrat, about 1 h 20 by car. The rest are easy swaps.

Chosen

Montserrat

~1 h 20 · by car

A jagged holy mountain: the Benedictine abbey, the Black Madonna, and a rack railway or cable car up. Drive out and park at the base.

GoogleApple

Sitges

~45 min · by car

A pretty beach town down the coast with calm swimming and a seafront promenade. Easy half day when everyone wants sand and gelato.

GoogleApple

Girona

~1 h 15 · by car

Medieval walls you can walk, a colourful riverfront, and the cathedral steps from Game of Thrones. An easy drive up the AP-7.

GoogleApple

Tarragona

~1 h 15 · by car

A Roman amphitheatre right by the sea, plus ruins scattered through town. History with a beach payoff for the kids.

GoogleApple
